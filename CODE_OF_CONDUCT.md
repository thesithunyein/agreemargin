# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect across issues, pull requests, and project discussions. Keep criticism specific and constructive. Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and opinions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone’s private information without permission are unacceptable.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a repository maintainer privately. Share enough context to help them investigate while respecting the privacy of everyone involved. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or restrict participation when necessary.
